WELLINGTON'S PREMIER TABLE TENNIS SQUAD

SPINSQUAD

Join SpinSquad Table Tennis, where juniors, adults and seniors come together to learn, train and improve their game.

At SpinSquad, we put structured, drill-based training at the heart of what we do. Rather than simply playing games, we focus on developing technique, consistency, movement and tactical skills through purposeful practice and repetition.

Whether your goal is to compete at a higher level or simply become a better player, SpinSquad offers a supportive environment where everyone can develop at their own pace and reach their potential.

COME AND PLAY

SESSIONS

We run regular training sessions catering to players of all ages and abilities.


Each session lasts two hours and combines structured drills, skill development and individual coaching. We focus on improving technique, consistency and match play through purposeful practice.

Sessions sometimes finish with a friendly doubles game, giving players the opportunity to put their skills into practice in a relaxed and enjoyable environment.
